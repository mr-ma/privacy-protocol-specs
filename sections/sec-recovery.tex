\subsection{Recovery Blob}
\label{sec:recovery}

\para{Definition.}
The recovery blob \blob\ lets the owner efficiently recover the plaintext balance.
It is a 16-byte one-time pad keyed by \dk\ and the current ciphertext:
%
\[
  \blob \;:=\; \HKDF(\dk;\, \cbal) \;\oplus\; b,
\]
%
where $b$ is the 128-bit balance.

\para{Decryption.}
The owner recomputes the 16-byte HKDF output from \dk\ and the on-chain ciphertext, then XORs to recover $b$.
The chain treats \blob\ as opaque and only enforces that the owner authorizes updates; an incorrect blob harms only its owner.
