\subsection{Benchmarks \new{(NEWLY ADDED)}}
\label{sec:benchmarks}

Measurements from the v4-sound reference implementation
(Ristretto255 backend, native release build),
end-to-end including bincode decoding.

\para{Verifier (chain-side).}
%
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l r r@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Op} & \textbf{verify (ms)} & \textbf{wire (B)} \\
  \midrule
  \texttt{configureAccount}     & $<\!1$  & $480$    \\
  \texttt{shield} (native)      & $0.97$  & $1{,}051$ \\
  \texttt{applyPending}         & $2.23$  & $1{,}824$ \\
  \texttt{unshield}             & $2.29$  & $1{,}796$ \\
  \texttt{transfer}             & $3.76$  & $2{,}407$ \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
%
Verify is mode-invariant within $\pm 0.05$\,ms across $\None$,
$\Capsule$, $\Authorize$, $\Both$; CE modes add $60$--$150$\,B to the
wire (CE sig and/or per-chunk capsule legs).
Verifier is reentrant; Bulletproof generators are immutable statics.

\para{Calldata.}
%
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=.5pt]
    \item Range proof (1{$\times$}128 + 4{$\times$}16 bits, batched): 16 IPA points $+$ 7 scalars $+$ 4 points $= 736$\,B.
    \item Per-chunk public data $(\Pchunk{i}, \Rsnd{i}, \Rrcv{i})$: $96$\,B; $\Capsule$ adds $\ctce{i}$ at $+32$\,B per chunk.
    \item DLEQ proof: $3$ scalars $= 96$\,B.
\end{itemize}

\para{Throughput.}
On Monad ($400$\,ms blocks, $2$\,MB calldata, $200$\,M gas) the
calldata ceiling at the measured wire is $2{,}000{,}000 / 2{,}407
\approx 831$ transfers/block, i.e.\ $\approx 2{,}078$ TPS.
Measured sustained on solonet: $\approx 1{,}885$ v4-sound TPS vs
$\approx 1{,}900$ native baseline on the same chain.
At $754$ transfers/block: calldata $1.81/2$\,MB, gas $151/200$\,M;
verify needs $\sim 7\times$ parallelism per $400$\,ms block.
Calldata saturates first, at $\approx 831$ transfers/block.

All numbers carry $\sim 2\times$ headroom over measured verify time;
the transfer charge is uniform across CE modes.

\para{Prover (client-side).}
Native release: $\sim 21$\,ms per transfer (any CE mode).
Browser WASM adds a constant factor; prover is off the chain critical
path and does not affect TPS.
