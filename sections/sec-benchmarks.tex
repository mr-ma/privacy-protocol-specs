\subsection{Benchmarks}
\label{sec:benchmarks}

Measurements are from the v4-sound reference implementation
(Ristretto255 backend, browser WASM prover, single-thread, M3),
end-to-end including bincode encoding.

\para{Calldata.}
%
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=.5pt]
    \item Range proof (1{$\times$}128 + 4{$\times$}16 bits, batched): 16 IPA points $+$ 7 scalars $+$ 4 points $= 736$\,B.
    \item Per-chunk public data $(\Pchunk{i}, \Rsnd{i}, \Rrcv{i})$: $96$\,B; $\Capsule$ adds $\ctce_i$ at $+32$\,B per chunk.
    \item DLEQ proof: $3$ scalars $= 96$\,B.
    \item Transfer total (4 chunks, Ristretto): $\sim 1.3$\,kB ($\Authorize$); $\sim 1.4$\,kB ($\Capsule$).
\end{itemize}

\para{Throughput.}
Under gas-limit billing a tx burns the full \texttt{gas\_limit}.
With a $50$\,M block and a $3$\,M transfer \texttt{gas\_limit} the
ceiling is $\sim 16$ private transfers per block; setting
\texttt{gas\_limit} from \texttt{tinyvm\_gas\_cost} $+\,50$k overhead
raises it to $\sim 80$/block.

\para{Decoder windows.}
Main-balance chunks are bounded by a single deposit, so BSGS over
$[0, 2^{32})$ per chunk is sufficient.
Pending chunks aggregate across senders up to $\pendcnt_{\max}$, so
the recovery procedure for pending uses a $2^{64}$ window.
Cold decode $<\!100$\,ms, warm $<\!1$\,ms with a $2^{16}$ baby-step
table.
A tighter $\pendcnt_{\max}$ (e.g.\ $16$ in v4-sound) keeps the
window bounded on resource-constrained wallets.
